About me

Hi! I'm Kabeer, an undergraduate at UC Berkeley studying Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS). I'm interested in digital design and computer architecture, and in building hardware for neurotechnology and medical devices.

I'm currently a Teaching Assistant for Berkeley's Great Ideas of Computer Architecture (CS 61C), and previously served as a Lab Tutor in Introduction to Circuits & Devices (EECS 16B). This past summer I was a Research Engineer Intern at Radiata, optimizing and speeding up transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) simulations for an Alzheimer's therapy. I am also an active member of Neurotech@Berkeley's Devices team where I worked on an EMG Wristband, and am currently working on a focused ultrasound project.

To learn more, check out my Experience, Projects, or Coursework.
